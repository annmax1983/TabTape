TabTape Privacy Policy

TabTape does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Recordings are captured, encoded and saved entirely on your device. The only network request the extension makes is the optional Pro licence check described in Section 4.

01Data Collection

TabTape does not collect any personal information, browsing data, page content or usage analytics. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or telemetry of any kind. Your recordings are yours: they are captured, encoded and stored on your device only.

02How TabTape Works

Everything happens inside your browser:

  • Capture — when you press Record, chrome.tabCapture takes the audio of the tab you chose, and only that tab. Other tabs, system sounds and the microphone are not captured.
  • Encoding — a hidden offscreen document encodes the audio in about one-second blocks (MP3 with the bundled lamejs encoder, or raw WAV).
  • Storage — each block is written into the extension's local database (IndexedDB) as it arrives, so memory stays flat and a crash cannot take the whole recording.
  • Output — pressing Stop assembles the file, keeps it in the library and downloads it to Downloads/TabTape/ when auto-download is on.
  • No page reading — there is no content script: TabTape never sees the HTML, text or form fields of the page you record.
At no point does audio or any other data leave your device.

04Third-Party Services & License Verification

TabTape does not connect to any third-party service, API, CDN or external server, and recording works entirely offline. The MP3 encoder (lamejs, LGPL) is bundled with the extension; nothing is fetched at runtime. The only exception is the optional Pro licence: free features make no network request at all. With a licence, the extension contacts our licence server (api.annmax1983.com) only to activate it, to re-verify it at most once every 2 hours when you open the popup, and to release it. These requests carry your licence key, a random install identifier, the plugin id and basic browser metadata (browser name/version, interface language, time zone, user agent) — never your recordings, page content or browsing data. Our licence server records the IP address of these requests solely for rate limiting and abuse prevention — never for profiling, advertising or sharing with third parties.

05Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data with third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

06Children's Privacy

TabTape does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

07What Is Captured — and What Is Not

  • Only the audio of the tab you started recording on. Microphone input and system audio are never captured.
  • DRM-protected players (subscription video, some music services) keep their audio away from extensions by design; those pages record as silence. That is the platform's protection doing its job.
  • Browser-internal pages (chrome://, the Web Store, the New Tab page) cannot be captured. Start on a normal http or https page.
  • No transcription, no summaries, no AI. TabTape produces an audio file and stops there.

08Data retention & deletion

Recordings live in the extension's local library and in your Downloads folder. Delete a recording in the library and the space is freed immediately; the downloaded copy stays yours. Uninstalling the extension removes its local library, so download the recordings you want to keep first. Nothing is retained on our servers, because your audio is never sent to them.
